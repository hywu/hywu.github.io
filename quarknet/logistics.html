Stipend

High-school science teachers are eligible for a stipend for the days they participate. To receive a stipend, please complete the following 3 steps:

1. Register here

2. Sign in each morning and afternoon. Sign out at the end of the workshop.

3. Complete the survey: use the full survey (if you didn't fill out a form 2023-25) or the short survey (if you filled out the form 2023-25).
